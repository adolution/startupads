7. Meta Pixel & Meta Conversions API

This website uses the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, 4 Grand Canal Square, Dublin 2, Ireland.

Meta Pixel (Client-Side)

The Meta Pixel is a JavaScript code snippet that captures conversion events in your browser (e.g., page view, button click, form submission). The data collected is transmitted to Meta and may be used to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to create Custom Audiences for retargeting campaigns.

Meta Conversions API (Server-Side)

In addition to the Pixel, we use the Meta Conversions API to send conversion events directly from our server to Meta. Data such as hashed email addresses, IP addresses, or browser identifiers (user agent) are transmitted in anonymised/hashed form. Identification of individual persons by Meta is not directly possible due to hashing.

Categories of Data Processed

  • IP address
  • Browser information (user agent)
  • Visited URLs and conversion events
  • Hashed email address (only upon form submission, SHA-256)
  • Click ID (fbclid) from Meta ads

Data Transfer to the USA

Meta Platforms Inc. (USA) may access the data. Meta is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. In addition, Standard Contractual Clauses have been agreed upon.

Legal Basis & Objection

The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may revoke your consent at any time via our cookie banner. Additionally, you can object to personalised advertising at facebook.com/adpreferences.

Meta Privacy Policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy
